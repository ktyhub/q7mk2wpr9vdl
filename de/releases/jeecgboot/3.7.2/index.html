Englisches Original / English original

JeecgBoot v3.7.2

Highlights

  • Free large-screen designer — Integrates JimuBI to provide a free dashboard and data-visualization designer, alongside an upgrade to the latest JimuReport. Token-aware, tenant-enabled dashboard access is also fixed (#7444), improving deployments that expose dashboards through authenticated links.
  • Redis compatibility and scalability — Replaces blocking KEYS lookups with SCAN (#31) and avoids KEYS in JeecgRedisCacheWriter (#6907), addressing clusters or managed Redis services that disable it (#6918). This is an important operational fix for production deployments; release notes also mention Redis subscription/startup and SAS serialization fixes.
  • Online ERP data-loss and reporting fixes — Addresses severe cases where editing a parent record in an online ERP form could delete all child-table data (#7190), plus child-table display/query issues and online-report field ordering, Chinese field names, and UNION support. These fixes materially improve the safety and usability of low-code form/report workflows.
  • Qiankun micro-frontend support — JeecgBoot can now serve as a qiankun sub-application, with related integration fixes (#6947, #7285). Teams embedding it in a Vue 3 micro-frontend shell should validate the matching qiankun integration and lifecycle configuration.
  • SAS/Spring Boot 3 reliability — Fixes token-refresh behavior that could unexpectedly log users out (#7305), @IgnoreAuth handling (#7459), XXL-JOB page errors, and online-form date formatting. SAS adopters should regression-test authentication and timezone-sensitive form rendering after upgrading.
  • Frontend and form-component corrections — Fixes FormProps propagation (#7150), grouped-header row editing (#7384), BasicTable selection/tree behavior, region linkage for Hong Kong/Macau, and XLSX template formulas after looping rows. Custom tables, generated ERP forms, and exports are the areas most worth targeted testing.
