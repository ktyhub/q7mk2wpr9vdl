LlamaFactory v0.9.2

Highlights

  • Training backend expansion — APOLLO (#6617) adds a memory-efficient optimizer, Ray Trainer (#6542, with ray_storage_path in #6920 and arg fixes in #6564/#6906) enables distributed training, and QLoRA lands on Ascend NPU (#6601). If you're scaling past single-GPU LoRA or running on Huawei hardware, this is the release that opens those paths — expect to rework launch configs when moving to Ray.
  • vLLM inference refreshed — Batch inference with tensor parallelism (#6190) is joined by vLLM bumps to 0.7.2 (#6857) then 0.7.3 (#7183, #7193), plus seed control (#7058) and batch-prediction fixes (#7235). Note the project pins vLLM (#6629), so a LLaMA-Factory upgrade can force a matching vLLM upgrade in your environment — verify compatibility before pinning.
  • Reasoning-model coverage — First-class templates for DeepSeek R1 (#6767) and V3 (671B), Qwen2.5-VL (#6779), Llama 3.3, Phi-4, QwQ, MiniCPM-V/o and Granite, plus reasoning SFT datasets (OpenO1, Open-Thoughts, Open-R1-Math, Chinese-DeepSeek-R1-Distill). You can now fine-tune distilled R1-style reasoning and the newest VLMs without hand-written templates.
  • Major internal refactors (migration watch-out) — VLM registration (#6600), mm plugin (#6895), template (#6896), data pipeline (#6901) and VLM arguments (#6976) were all rewritten, with experimental chat-template parsing straight from the tokenizer (#6905). Custom multimodal plugins, templates or data pipelines built against 0.9.1 will likely need reworking — validate configs on a staging branch before upgrading.
  • Tooling and dependency upgrades — transformers moves 4.48 (#6628) → 4.49 (#6982), WebUI jumps to Gradio 5 (#6688), tokenizers is pinned (#6157), and uv run is supported (#6907). New hooks include SwanLab tracking (#6401), Ollama modelfile auto-generation (#4686), trust_remote_code (#5819) and ALLOW_EXTRA_ARGS (#6831). The Gradio 5 and transformers bumps are the likeliest sources of extension breakage.
  • Git history rewritten with BFG — Large files were purged from repository history; a backup lives at hiyouga/LLaMA-Factory-backup. Existing clones and forks won't fast-forward — re-clone or rebase from the new history before pulling, or you'll hit conflicts and keep the bloated objects.
