OmniRoute v3.8.44

Highlights

  • Codex quota protection under concurrency — #6009 adds a global minimum-interval gate ahead of actual Codex quota network calls, covering concurrent Auto-Combo/preflight traffic that the periodic bulk sync did not serialize. This reduces the risk of OAuth-token revocation when many accounts share an IP. Cache hits remain immediate, and failures fail open; tune with OMNIROUTE_QUOTA_FETCH_MIN_INTERVAL_MS (default 250ms, range 0–5000ms).
  • Request-level routing and budget controls — #6024 adds X-OmniRoute-Mode and X-OmniRoute-Budget so callers can override an auto combo’s scoring mode and impose a hard per-request USD ceiling without changing stored configuration. Presets include fast, balanced, quality, cheap, reliable, and offline; raw mode-pack names are also accepted. Invalid values are ignored, making these headers suitable for untrusted or heterogeneous clients.
  • New endpoints and modality support — Mistral OCR is available through /v1/ocr with its own OCR provider category, alongside new Mistral moderation support (#5950). The read-only provider-plugin manifest at GET /api/v1/provider-plugin-manifest gives sidecars and relays a discoverable provider inventory; Bifrost and CLIProxyAPI can receive its URL through X-OmniRoute-Provider-Manifest-Url (#6001, #6007).
  • Local-only provider discovery, persisted behind the dashboard — #5939 adds durable discovery results, upsert de-duplication on (provider, method, endpoint), and /api/discovery/* scan/verify/delete routes. The service is opt-in and default-off; every route is strictly loopback-only and cannot be reached through tunnels or manage-scope bypass. The dashboard tab supports reviewing and verifying findings, but deployment topology must preserve direct local access.
  • Broader gateway and local-runtime support — BYOK gateways include Kenari, SumoPod, X5Lab, Charm Hyper, Nube.sh, b.ai, Qiniu, and ModelScope, with live /v1/models discovery replacing static catalogs for several providers. Antigravity now includes claude-sonnet-5 (#6103). Bifrost and Mux become supervised embedded services with loopback-only lifecycle APIs and dashboard tabs (#5817, #6034).
  • Cleaner reasoning output and deployment ergonomics — MiniMax M3’s raw <think> content is extracted into reasoning_content across the eight OpenAI-format tiers, while native Anthropic-format MiniMax tiers remain unchanged (#6073, #6050); clients parsing content may observe less thinking leakage. Reverse-proxy subpath deployments can set OMNIROUTE_BASE_PATH instead of manually stripping headers (#5992), and backend-only builds can skip dashboard compilation to shorten CI and local build cycles (#6119).

No breaking changes identified in the provided changelog.
