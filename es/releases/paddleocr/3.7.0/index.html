#PaddleOCR v3.7.0

2026.6.11 v3.7.0 lanzado

  • Lanzamiento PP-OCRv6

    • Aumento de precisión: el nivel medio logra un +4.6% de detección y un +5.1% de reconocimiento sobre PP-OCRv5_server, superando a los VLM convencionales (Qwen3-VL-235B, GPT-5.5) con solo 34,5 millones de parámetros.
    • 50 idiomas unificados: el modelo único cubre chino, inglés, japonés y 46 idiomas de escritura latina; no es necesario cambiar de modelo.
    • Escenarios especializados: mejoras importantes en pantallas digitales, caracteres de matriz de puntos, huellas de neumáticos y reconocimiento de texto industrial.
    • Inferencia más rápida: aceleración de CPU 5.2× (OpenVINO), 6.1× en Apple M4 (pequeño), 0,13 s en GPU A100.
    • Tres niveles para todos los escenarios: pequeño (1,5 millones) / pequeño (7,7 millones) / mediano (34,5 millones) para implementación perimetral, móvil y de servidor.

PaddleOCR v3.7.0

Highlights

  • New PP-OCRv6 model family — The medium tier improves detection by 4.6% and recognition by 5.1% over PP-OCRv5_server with 34.5M parameters. Validate these vendor-reported gains on your own documents before promoting it to production.
  • Three deployment tiers — Choose tiny (1.5M parameters), small (7.7M), or medium (34.5M) for edge, mobile, or server workloads respectively. This makes model selection an explicit accuracy-versus-footprint decision.
  • Unified 50-language model — A single model covers Chinese, English, Japanese, and 46 Latin-script languages, eliminating language-specific model switching in multilingual pipelines. Confirm coverage for your actual languages and scripts.
  • Improved industrial text recognition — PP-OCRv6 targets digital displays, dot-matrix characters, tire prints, and industrial markings—scenarios where conventional OCR and general-purpose VLMs often struggle.
  • Backend-specific performance gains — Reported results include 5.2× faster medium-tier CPU inference with OpenVINO, 6.1× faster tiny-tier inference on Apple M4, and 0.13-second A100 inference. These use different tiers and hardware, so benchmark them separately rather than treating them as one comparable speedup.
  • Adoption strategy — Keep PP-OCRv5_server as a baseline, evaluate each PP-OCRv6 tier on representative production images, and verify accuracy and latency under your deployment backend before switching.
  • Breaking changes — None announced.

2026.6.11 v3.7.0 released

  • Release PP-OCRv6

    • Accuracy boost: Medium tier achieves +4.6% detection and +5.1% recognition over PP-OCRv5_server, surpassing mainstream VLMs (Qwen3-VL-235B, GPT-5.5) with only 34.5M parameters.
    • 50 languages unified: Single model covers Chinese, English, Japanese, and 46 Latin-script languages — no model switching needed.
    • Specialized scenarios: Major improvements in digital displays, dot-matrix characters, tire prints, and industrial text recognition.
    • Faster inference: 5.2× CPU speedup (OpenVINO), 6.1× on Apple M4 (tiny), 0.13s on A100 GPU.
    • Three tiers for all scenarios: tiny (1.5M) / small (7.7M) / medium (34.5M) for edge, mobile, and server deployment.

2026.6.11 v3.7.0 发布

  • 发布 PP-OCRv6

    • 精度全面提升：medium 档相比 PP-OCRv5_server 检测精度提升 4.6%、识别精度提升 5.1%，以仅 34.5M 参数超越 Qwen3-VL-235B、GPT-5.5 等主流视觉语言大模型。
    • 50 种语言统一支持：单一模型覆盖中文、英文、日文及 46 种拉丁语系语言，无需为不同语种切换模型。
    • 专业场景增强：数码显示屏、点阵字符、轮胎印字、工业字符等传统 VLM 难以覆盖的场景识别能力大幅提升。
    • 推理速度更快：medium 档 CPU OpenVINO 推理加速 5.2×，tiny 档 Apple M4 加速 6.1×，A100 上仅需 0.13s。
    • 三档模型覆盖全场景：tiny（1.5M）/ small（7.7M）/ medium（34.5M）分别面向端侧/移动端/服务端部署。
