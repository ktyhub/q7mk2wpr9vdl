  • [Backport 2.6] feat(volume): external volume and describe_volume by @lentitude2tk in https://github.com/milvus-io/pymilvus/pull/3455

  • [Backport 2.6] refactor: remove gRPC handler _get_info helpers by @pymilvus-bot in https://github.com/milvus-io/pymilvus/pull/3463
  • [Backport 2.6] feat: add ORM API deprecation warnings (#3467) by @pymilvus-bot in https://github.com/milvus-io/pymilvus/pull/3468
