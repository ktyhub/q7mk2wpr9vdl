acelerar v1.13.0

Soporte para neuronas de AWS

Ahora tenemos soporte para dispositivos AWS Neuron (Trainium/Inferentia). Gracias @michaelbenayoun por agregar esto.

  • Integración de neuronas por @michaelbenayoun en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3935

Mejoras en XPU

Eliminamos la dependencia de IPEX y mejoramos el código independiente del dispositivo para XPU.

  • usando spawn en lugar de fork para el dispositivo XPU por @kaixuanliu en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3884
  • Eliminar ipex por @yao-matrix en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3883
  • mejorar nuevos códigos para XPU y hacerlos independientes del dispositivo por @yao-matrix en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3890
  • Se corrigió KMP_AFFINITY configurado incorrectamente para entrenamiento sin CPU por @hexfaker en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3912

Mejoras de FSDP2

Hemos agregado un montón de correcciones importantes para los usuarios de FSDP2: actualización solo de parámetros que requieren graduación, errores de incrustación mejor vinculados, carga del optimizador DCP, corrección de fallos del paso del optimizador bf16 y compatibilidad con antorcha < 2.7.0.

  • Actualización de parámetros FSDP2 solo si require_grad por @ojh31 en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3848
  • Se corrigieron errores de incrustación relacionados con FSDP2 con la guía de ValueError dirigida por @amanzoni1 en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3878
  • error: fsdp no puede cargar el estado del optimizador usando dcp por @flymin en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3904
  • Se solucionó el problema en el optimizador.step cuando fsdp2 está habilitado y el modelo es bfloat16 por @sywangyi en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3905
  • Se corrigió el fallo de FSDP2 con ignore_params en la antorcha < 2.7.0 por @Mr-Neutr0n en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3924

Paralelismo de secuencia DeepSpeed

Agregamos varias correcciones a la integración de DeepSpeed + Sequence Parallelism introducida en v1.12.0, incluido el soporte de evaluación durante la capacitación de SP y el manejo adecuado del grupo de procesos.

  • [SP] Corrige el ejemplo de cálculo de pérdidas realizado por @kashif en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3858
  • Error [SP y CP] si tanto CP como SP están habilitados por @kashif en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3862
  • DeepSpeed tiene su propio grupo de procesos de @kashif en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3916
  • [Deepspeed] omite la creación de malla del dispositivo cuando deepspeed y sp_size >1 por @kashif en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3914
  • Habilitar la evaluación durante la secuencia paralela de velocidad profunda por @jp1924 en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3917

8PM

Hemos mejorado la formación del 8PM. Gracias @shimizust por arreglar el soporte de torchao.

  • Se corrigió la configuración predeterminada de FP8 torchao con relleno y soporte completo para FSDP2 por @shimizust en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3831
  • Se corrigió la ejecución con Transformer Engine por @ksivaman en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3852

Accelerate ahora importa más rápido al aplazar las dependencias importantes y los ganchos de torch.compile se desactivan de forma diferida.

  • Importación más rápida por @SunMarc en https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3953

accelerate v1.13.0

Highlights

  • AWS Neuron joins the accelerator roster — accelerate now natively targets Trainium/Inferentia via a new Neuron backend (#3935). If you're running on AWS silicon, you can drop custom device-plumbing and use the standard Accelerator/AcceleratorConfig flow; expect to validate mixed-precision and distributed settings against Neuron's own plugin behavior, since this is a first integration rather than a mature one.
  • XPU goes dependency-free and device-agnostic — The IPEX dependency is removed (#3883) and XPU code paths were rewritten to be device-agnostic (#3890), with spawn replacing fork for XPU multiprocessing (#3884). XPU users should remove explicit ipex pins from their environments and re-test dataloader workers: the fork→spawn switch can surface pickling/global-state issues that were previously hidden. Also watch #3912 — a bad KMP_AFFINITY setting was being applied to non-CPU runs, so affinity-sensitive CPU/GPU jobs may see different threading behavior after upgrading.
  • FSDP2 gets production-hardened — A cluster of fixes removes sharp edges for FSDP2 adopters: params are upcast only when requires_grad (#3848), tied-embedding failures now emit a targeted ValueError (#3878), DCP loading of optimizer state works (#3904), a bf16 optimizer.step() crash is fixed (#3905), and ignored_params no longer breaks on torch < 2.7.0 (#3924). Net effect: bf16 + tied-embedding + checkpoint-resume setups that previously crashed are now viable, and users pinned below torch 2.7 no longer need to bump just for FSDP2.
  • DeepSpeed sequence parallelism becomes usable for real training loops — The v1.12.0 SP integration gains evaluation support during SP runs (#3917) and its own dedicated process group (#3916), with device-mesh creation skipped when DeepSpeed and sp_size > 1 are combined (#3914). Two guardrails matter: enabling context parallelism and sequence parallelism together now errors out explicitly (#3862), and the loss-computation example was corrected (#3858) — check your custom loss scaling against the updated reference.
  • FP8 tightens up, MS-AMP on notice — torchao's default FP8 config is fixed for padding and FSDP2 all-gather support (#3831), and Transformer Engine execution is repaired (#3852). MS-AMP now emits deprecation warnings (#3857, docs in #3929), so if it's in your low-precision stack, start planning a migration — it's still functional, but flagged for removal.
  • Faster startup and safer data handling — Heavy imports are deferred so import accelerate is noticeably quicker (#3953), and torch.compile hooks are now disabled lazily (#3947, #3888), reducing overhead for non-compiled runs. On the data side, dispatch_batches=True now tolerates non-tensor batch values (#3850) and the stateful dataloader's KeyError with num_workers > 0 is fixed (#3931) along with its DDP behavior (#3952) — worth a look if you resume training from saved dataloader state in multi-worker distributed jobs.

No breaking changes are reported in this release.

AWS Neuron support

We now have support for AWS Neuron (Trainium/Inferentia) devices. Thanks @michaelbenayoun for adding this.

  • Neuron integration by @michaelbenayoun in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3935

XPU Improvements

We've removed IPEX dependency and improved device-agnostic code for XPU.

  • using spawn instead of fork for XPU device by @kaixuanliu in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3884
  • Remove ipex by @yao-matrix in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3883
  • enhance new codes to XPU, and make them be device agnostic by @yao-matrix in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3890
  • Fix KMP_AFFINITY incorrectly set for non-CPU training by @hexfaker in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3912

FSDP2 Improvements

We've added a bunch of important fixes for FSDP2 users: upcasting only grad-requiring params, better tied embedding errors, DCP optimizer loading, bf16 optimizer step crash fix, and torch < 2.7.0 compatibility.

  • Upcast FSDP2 parameters only if requires_grad by @ojh31 in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3848
  • Fix FSDP2 tied embedding errors with targeted ValueError guidance by @amanzoni1 in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3878
  • bug: fsdp cannot load optimizer state using dcp by @flymin in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3904
  • fix crash in optimizer.step when fsdp2 is enabled and model is bfloat16 by @sywangyi in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3905
  • Fix FSDP2 crash with ignored_params on torch < 2.7.0 by @Mr-Neutr0n in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3924

DeepSpeed Sequence Parallelism

We've added several fixes to the DeepSpeed + Sequence Parallelism integration introduced in v1.12.0, including evaluation support during SP training and proper process group handling.

  • [SP] fix loss computation example by @kashif in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3858
  • [SP and CP] error out if both CP and SP enabled by @kashif in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3862
  • DeepSpeed has its own process group by @kashif in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3916
  • [Deepspeed] skip device mesh creation when deepspeed and sp_size >1 by @kashif in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3914
  • Enable evaluation during deepspeed Sequence Parallel by @jp1924 in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3917

FP8

We've enhanced FP8 training. Thanks @shimizust for fixing torchao support.

  • Fix FP8 torchao default config with padding and FSDP2 all-gather support by @shimizust in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3831
  • Fix execution with Transformer Engine by @ksivaman in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3852
  • add MS-AMP deprecation warnings by @neha222222 in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3857

Performance

Accelerate now imports faster by deferring heavy dependencies, and torch.compile hooks are disabled lazily.

  • Faster import by @SunMarc in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3953
  • lazy compile disable by @SunMarc in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3947
  • Disable hook compile by @SunMarc in https://github.com/huggingface/accelerate/pull/3888
